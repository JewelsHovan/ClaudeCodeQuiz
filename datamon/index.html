Overworld: move with arrow keys, WASD, or click and hold in a direction. Q opens the Question Hub to practice missed answers, browse all questions, and find colleagues. Escape clears walking directions. Dialogue: Enter or Space reveals and advances, arrow keys choose, number keys select, and Escape skips or closes. Agent Operations: use arrow keys to focus, number keys 1 through 4 or Enter to select, R to run, and M to mute.
